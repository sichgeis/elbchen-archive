# AI-Generated Research Reports
This is a curated list of automatically generated research reports to various topics. The reports are the unalteded results of an AI system that is invoked with a research query provided by a human.

The research is coducted as an agentic workflow leveraging automated websearch and large languge model inference. The implementation is an adaption of the excellent [GPT Researcher](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher).
